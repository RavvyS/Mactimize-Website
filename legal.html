Privacy Policy

Mactimize is built to run 100% locally on your Mac. We do not collect, transmit, or sell your personal files, folder names, system diagnostics, or usage analytics.

  • Local Scanning: Cache, duplicate, startup item, memory, and Apple SMC fan scans happen strictly on-device.
  • License Activation & Updates: When you activate a license key or check for updates, Mactimize sends only your license key and Mac device name to our payment provider (Dodo Payments) or reads public release metadata from GitHub Releases.
  • Payments: All checkout and billing data is processed securely by Dodo Payments as our Merchant of Record. We never see or store your card details.

Terms of Service

By purchasing or using Mactimize, you receive a personal, non-exclusive, lifetime license to install and use Mactimize on your macOS devices (up to the device activation limit specified at checkout).

  • Safety & Trash: Mactimize moves cleaned files to your macOS Trash rather than permanently erasing them, except when you explicitly choose to empty Trash. Always review items marked “Review” before removing them.
  • Updates: Lifetime licenses include all future v1.x updates and maintenance releases for macOS 13 Ventura and newer.
  • Acceptable Use: You may not redistribute, resell, or share your license key publicly.

14-Day Refund Policy

We want you to love Mactimize. If Mactimize does not work as expected on your Mac or you are unsatisfied for any reason, you can request a full refund within 14 days of purchase.

To request a refund, reply to your Dodo Payments order receipt email with your order ID or license key. Once processed, your license key will be deactivated and your full payment will be returned to your original payment method.
