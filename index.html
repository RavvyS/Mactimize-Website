Mactimize Safety Guide

Every item in Mactimize is labeled before you touch it:

  • Safe: Caches, SHA-256 duplicates, and inactive project builds. Moves to macOS Trash.
  • Review: Large files, active builds, and background daemons. Never auto-selected.
  • Keep: Apple system caches and your active workspace. Always protected.
