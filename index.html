Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Will cleaning browser caches log me out or erase bookmarks?

No. Mactimize only cleans temporary media & script caches in ~/Library/Caches. Your logins, cookies, passwords, and bookmarks are stored in Application Support and remain completely untouched.

Does Mactimize permanently delete files?

No. All cleaned caches, duplicates, and user LaunchAgents are moved safely to the macOS Trash via FileManager.trashItem so you can restore anything anytime with Finder's “Put Back”.

How does fan control work on fanless MacBook Air vs MacBook Pro?

Mactimize reads FNum directly from AppleSMC. On MacBook Pro it displays Left & Right fan sliders and presets; on fanless Macs it automatically shows “Fanless Mac Detected” while still displaying all live thermal sensors.

Why does macOS ask to confirm on first launch?

Because Mactimize is a free, open-source indie build without a paid Apple notarization certificate, macOS Gatekeeper may ask you to confirm on the first launch. Simply right-click Mactimize.app in Applications and click Open, or run xattr -cr /Applications/Mactimize.app.

Read-First Safety Model

Every action in Mactimize is engineered to protect your data and active work:

  • macOS Trash Only: Never runs destructive rm -rf — restore anything from Trash.
  • Apple CryptoKit SHA-256: Duplicate files are verified byte-for-byte with the original copy always kept.
  • Active Project Shield: Recently modified node_modules and target/ folders stay unchecked by default.
